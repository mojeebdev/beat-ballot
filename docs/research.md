# Beat Ballot research ledger

## Editorial position

Beat Ballot is a fan-led response to the current Olamide/Davido hit-for-hit conversation. It must never present itself as an official battle, a label-led campaign, or an artist endorsement.

The main ballot uses lead and co-lead records only. Featured appearances, remixes and shared records belong in a later Feature Hall so no artist is advantaged by a different definition of authorship. `The Money` (Davido × Olamide) is deliberately excluded from either side of the primary ballot.

## What is evidenced

The song index stores three evidence levels:

- `chart record` — a specific chart, stream, or certification milestone is linked.
- `catalogue context` — a record appears in reputable career/cultural catalogue coverage, without invented comparative numbers.
- `cultural marker` — an intentionally modest description of the song's visible cultural moment.

## Starting matchups

1. **The Ignition** — `Eni Duro` / `Dami Duro`: early breakout statements.
2. **The Dancefloor** — `Durosoke` / `Skelewu`: 2013, movement-first records.
3. **The Phrase** — `Who You Epp?` / `IF`: songs that escaped into everyday language.
4. **The Return** — `Loading` / `FEM`: 2020 reset records.
5. **The Next Wave** — `Amapiano` / `Unavailable`: newer collaborations with chart evidence.

The matchups are an editorial lens, not a claim of numerical equivalence. Fans vote on the record they would personally defend.
